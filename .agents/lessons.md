- 2026-09-03: When preparing game-agent demos, start with a real existing game or a recognizable, complete human play loop. Do not invent a score quota or victory setup to make the agent experiment succeed. Protocol tests and game art do not establish enjoyment. Owner rejected the bespoke RPG/RTS direction twice; inspect and play the standalone game before freezing experiments. A disabled WebMCP toggle is not a no-integration code baseline.
- 2026-09-03: When a game depends on movement, aiming, cards, maps, or other spatial interaction, human controls must use the medium naturally: keyboard/pointer on desktop and direct canvas taps, drags, swipes, or multitouch on mobile. A bank of command buttons may remain as an accessibility or discoverability fallback, but it must not be the primary play surface. Agent-facing semantic actions belong in WebMCP, not in a human button wall.
